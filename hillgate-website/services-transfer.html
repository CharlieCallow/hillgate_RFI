Transfer services for crypto-assets

Hillgate provides transfer services for electronic money tokens on behalf of professional clients.

Subject to authorisation, this service will be provided by Hillgate Finance B.V. as a Crypto-Asset Service Provider under Regulation (EU) 2023/1114. Authorisation pending with the Autoriteit Financiële Markten.
Hillgate provides services to professional and business clients only.

What this service covers

On client instruction, Hillgate transfers EMTs from a client's Hillgate wallet to a third-party wallet address (a beneficiary). Hillgate does not provide consumer-style on-chain wallets to retail users.

Beneficiary controls

Beneficiary wallets are subject to onboarding, screening and ongoing monitoring. Transfers to a non-trusted beneficiary require strong customer authentication and may be subject to additional review.

Travel rule and supervisory expectations

Hillgate complies with Regulation (EU) 2023/1113 on information accompanying transfers of funds and certain crypto-assets. Required originator and beneficiary information is collected and transmitted in line with the regulation.

Networks and supported assets

Currently USDC and EURC, on the networks listed in the client application at the time of transfer initiation.
