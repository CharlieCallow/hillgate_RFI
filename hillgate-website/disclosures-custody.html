Custody policy

Public-facing custody policy under Article 75 of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 (MiCAR).

Scope

This policy applies to crypto-assets held by Hillgate on behalf of clients in the course of providing the custody and administration service.

Segregation

Client crypto-assets are held through Stichting Custody Hillgate Finance, a Dutch foundation with the sole purpose of holding client crypto-assets. The foundation is bankruptcy-remote from Hillgate. Client crypto-assets are not part of Hillgate's own balance sheet.

Internal allocation

At the level of the foundation, client crypto-assets are held in omnibus wallets. Hillgate maintains a per-client internal allocation in its ledger. Reconciliation between the internal ledger and on-chain balances is performed daily.

No use of client assets

Hillgate does not pledge, lend, rehypothecate, stake, or otherwise use client crypto-assets for its own account or for the account of another client.

Wallet infrastructure

Wallet keys are held with Circle Internet Financial, a regulated digital asset infrastructure provider. Operational controls include strong customer authentication for client-initiated withdrawals and four-eyes review for non-trusted destinations.

Insolvency protection

In the event of Hillgate's insolvency, client crypto-assets held by the foundation remain segregated and are not available to Hillgate's creditors.

Reporting

Clients can view their crypto-asset balances and transaction history in the client application at any time.
