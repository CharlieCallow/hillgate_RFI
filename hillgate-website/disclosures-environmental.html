Environmental impact of consensus mechanisms

Disclosure under Article 66(5) of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 (MiCAR).

Hillgate is required to publish information on the principal adverse impacts on the climate and other environment-related impacts of the consensus mechanisms used to issue the crypto-assets in relation to which it provides services. The supported list and underlying networks are summarised below.

Supported crypto-assets and networks

Crypto-assetIssuerNative blockchainConsensus mechanismNetwork energy profile
USDCCircle Internet FinancialEthereum (and other authorised networks)Proof of Stake (Ethereum) and othersLow. Ethereum's transition to Proof of Stake reduced its energy consumption by approximately 99.9% relative to Proof of Work.
EURCCircle Internet FinancialEthereum (and other authorised networks)Proof of Stake (Ethereum) and othersLow, as above.

Methodology and information sources

The information above is drawn from the issuer's white paper, the technical documentation of the underlying networks, and publicly available environmental impact assessments. The full set of mandatory environmental indicators required under Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/422 (the environmental impact RTS) is included in the issuer's white paper, which is linked from the supported asset entry above. Hillgate updates this page when the supported list of crypto-assets changes.

Hillgate's own footprint

As a service provider, Hillgate's own infrastructure runs on cloud services that publish environmental disclosures. Hillgate's primary infrastructure provider is DigitalOcean. Hillgate considers the environmental footprint of its infrastructure providers as part of its outsourcing due diligence.
