Exchange of crypto-assets

Hillgate offers exchange services to professional clients, exchanging electronic money tokens for funds, and electronic money tokens for other electronic money tokens, using its proprietary capital.

Subject to authorisation, this service will be provided by Hillgate Finance B.V. as a Crypto-Asset Service Provider under Regulation (EU) 2023/1114. Authorisation pending with the Autoriteit Financiële Markten.
Hillgate provides services to professional and business clients only.

How the exchange works

Hillgate acts as principal counterparty. The client requests a quote. Once the client accepts, Hillgate is contractually bound to that price, regardless of liquidity provider availability or behaviour. The client faces Hillgate; the client does not face any liquidity provider.

Single all-in price

A USDC to EUR trade is economically a redemption from USDC into USD followed by an FX leg into EUR, but the client sees one contract with Hillgate at one all-in price. The FX leg runs on Hillgate's books under its EMI authorisation. The crypto leg runs under MiCAR.

Public firm prices

Hillgate publishes firm reference prices for all supported pairs without registration, in line with Article 77(2) MiCAR. Tier-based markups are also public. See our Pricing page.

Hillgate carries the risk

Hillgate bears any basis risk between the price quoted to the client and the cost of any subsequent hedging on its own balance sheet. Whether and how Hillgate hedges is at Hillgate's discretion within its risk management framework, and does not affect the price the client receives.
