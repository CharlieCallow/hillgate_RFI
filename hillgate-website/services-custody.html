Custody and administration of crypto-assets

Hillgate offers segregated custody of electronic money tokens (USDC and EURC) on behalf of professional clients. Crypto-assets are held through a dedicated foundation, Stichting Custody Hillgate Finance, segregated from Hillgate's own balance sheet.

Subject to authorisation, this service will be provided by Hillgate Finance B.V. as a Crypto-Asset Service Provider under Regulation (EU) 2023/1114. Authorisation pending with the Autoriteit Financiële Markten.
Hillgate provides services to professional and business clients only.

What we hold

Currently USDC and EURC. The list of supported electronic money tokens may be expanded over time and will be reflected on this page.

How we hold it

Client EMTs are held in omnibus wallets at the level of Stichting Custody Hillgate Finance, with internal allocation per client recorded in Hillgate's ledger. Clients are entitled to their crypto-assets at all times. Hillgate does not pledge, lend, rehypothecate or otherwise use client crypto-assets for its own account.

Operational safeguards

Wallet keys are held with a regulated digital asset infrastructure provider (Circle). Withdrawal flows are subject to strong customer authentication and four-eyes review for non-trusted destinations. Reconciliations are performed daily.
