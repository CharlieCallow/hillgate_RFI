Pricing

Hillgate publishes its pricing methodology in a prominent place on its website, in compliance with Article 66(4) and Article 77 of MiCAR. Live firm prices and post-trade transparency information are published at prices.hillgate.com without registration.

Hillgate provides services to professional and business clients only.

Methodology

Hillgate's price for an exchange transaction is constructed from two elements: a published reference mid price, and a tier-based markup. Both are publicly available and verifiable by any client.

Reference mid prices

Hillgate uses Bloomberg BFIX rates as the reference mid for the underlying currency pair. For redemption between an EMT and its reference fiat currency (for example USDC and USD), an additional EMT redemption parameter is applied. During non-market hours, the reference price is calculated as the most recent business-day close plus or minus 50 basis points.

Tier-based markups (non-discriminatory commercial policy)

Hillgate operates a published, objective and non-discriminatory tier structure based on measurable criteria including trading volume, average ticket size, KYC tier and length of relationship. The tier and markup applicable to a client are stated in the client agreement.

Payments and FX (EMI) pricing

Hillgate's EMI services are priced on a flat-fee schedule, set out in the client agreement and the Hillgate fee schedule. The schedule is shared during onboarding and is available on request to existing clients.

Post-trade transparency

In line with Article 77(4) MiCAR, Hillgate publishes information on every executed exchange transaction within one business day at prices.hillgate.com. Disclosures include trade volume, executed price and the prescribed transaction metadata. The format follows Annex II, Table 2 of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/417.
