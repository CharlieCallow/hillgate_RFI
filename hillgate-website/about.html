About Hillgate

Hillgate Finance B.V. was incorporated in December 2024 in Amsterdam. It is a Dutch besloten vennootschap that holds an Electronic Money Institution licence from De Nederlandsche Bank and has applied to the Autoriteit Financiële Markten for authorisation as a Crypto-Asset Service Provider under MiCAR.

Hillgate provides services to professional and business clients only. Hillgate does not onboard retail clients.

Mission

Hillgate's mission is to address the operational, banking and foreign exchange challenges faced by institutional participants in the digital asset sector. We provide reliable, compliant and efficient payment, FX and (subject to authorisation) crypto-asset services that meet the needs of regulated institutional users.

What we do

Hillgate operates an integrated platform combining e-money, foreign exchange and (upon authorisation) crypto-asset services for institutional users.

  • An instant settlement network for fiat between Hillgate clients, twenty-four hours a day, including weekends and public holidays.
  • API-enabled foreign exchange trading across 14 fiat currencies, with firm prices at the moment of acceptance.
  • Upon MiCAR authorisation, custody, exchange and transfer services for electronic money tokens (currently USDC and EURC).

Background

Hillgate was founded by a working group from Forte Securities Limited, an FCA-regulated London inter-dealer brokerage. Hillgate operates as an independent brand and a separate legal entity, with its own organisational structure, IT infrastructure and service model. Forte Securities is not authorised to sell or promote Hillgate services.

Where we operate

Hillgate operates from Keizersgracht 391A, 1016 EJ Amsterdam. Services are provided in English. Hillgate's services are accessible to clients established in member states of the European Economic Area, subject to onboarding and risk acceptance.
