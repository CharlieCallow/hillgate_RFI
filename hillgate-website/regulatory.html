How to verify our status

Hillgate's EMI authorisation can be verified directly in the DNB register at dnb.nl. Hillgate's MiCAR authorisation, once granted, will be verifiable in the AFM register at afm.nl and in the ESMA register of CASPs.

Applicable rules

Hillgate operates in compliance with the Dutch Financial Supervision Act (Wft), the Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing Act (Wwft), the EU Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and, upon authorisation, MiCAR and its delegated regulations.

Group context

Hillgate operates as an independent legal entity. Forte Securities Limited is a passive minority shareholder. Forte Securities Limited is not authorised to sell, market or promote Hillgate's services.
