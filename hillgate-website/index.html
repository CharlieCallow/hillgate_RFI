Regulated financial infrastructure for institutional digital asset participants.

Hillgate is an Amsterdam-based Electronic Money Institution offering payments, foreign exchange and, subject to authorisation, crypto-asset services to professional and business clients in the digital asset sector.

Our services Regulatory framework
Hillgate provides services to professional and business clients only. Hillgate does not onboard retail clients.
What Hillgate offers

Two regulated tracks, one platform.

EMI · DNB

Payments and FX

Multi-currency named accounts, third-party payments, foreign exchange across 14 currencies. Provided under our DNB Electronic Money Institution licence.

MiCAR · AFM (pending)

Crypto-asset custody and exchange

Custody, administration, exchange and transfer of electronic money tokens. Provided under MiCAR upon authorisation by the AFM.

Single integrated platform

One client agreement, one interface, one set of operational controls covering both regulated activities. Clients see fiat and EMT positions side by side.

Built for institutional users

Who we serve and how.

  • Institutional only

    Hillgate exclusively onboards regulated institutions and professional clients. Vast majority of clients are EEA-regulated counterparties including CASPs, MiFID II investment firms, EMIs and equivalents.

  • EMT-focused

    The crypto-assets supported are limited to electronic money tokens (currently USDC and EURC). No volatile cryptocurrencies, no privacy coins.

  • Compliance first

    Three lines of defence model, independent Chief Compliance Officer, full alignment with DORA, Wwft and MiCAR governance expectations.
