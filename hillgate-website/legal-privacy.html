Privacy notice

Hillgate Finance B.V. is the data controller for personal data collected through this website and through the provision of its services. This notice sets out how Hillgate handles personal data.

Categories of personal data

Identification data (name, date of birth, government ID), contact data, transactional data, device and usage data when you visit this website, KYC and due diligence data for clients and their representatives, communications and complaints data.

Purposes

Onboarding and client due diligence, providing services, transaction monitoring, compliance with anti-money laundering, sanctions and tax obligations, security and fraud prevention, communicating with you, improving our services, complying with our legal and regulatory obligations.

Legal bases

Performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, our legitimate interests, and where applicable consent.

Recipients

Hillgate's regulated and contracted service providers (banking, custody, KYC, IT infrastructure), competent authorities where required by law, and professional advisors.

Retention

Personal data is retained for the period required by applicable law, including the five-year retention requirement under the Wwft, and longer where required.

Your rights

You have rights of access, rectification, erasure (subject to legal retention obligations), restriction, objection, and data portability. To exercise these, contact privacy@hillgate.com.

Complaints

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Dutch Data Protection Authority).
