Crypto-asset risk warning

Disclosure under Article 66(3) of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 (MiCAR).

Hillgate is required to warn clients about the risks associated with transactions in crypto-assets. Please read this warning carefully before engaging with any of Hillgate's crypto-asset services.

Crypto-assets are a highly volatile and emerging class of asset. The value of a crypto-asset can rise or fall significantly in short periods of time. You may lose some or all of the funds you commit to a crypto-asset transaction.

Specific risks

  • Price risk

    The market value of a crypto-asset can change rapidly, including outside normal market hours. Even electronic money tokens that are designed to maintain a stable value may, in stress scenarios, deviate from their reference.

  • Issuer and reserve risk

    An electronic money token relies on the issuer and the assets held in reserve. The credit, operational and legal standing of the issuer and the custodians of the reserve are relevant to the holder's position.

  • Operational and technology risk

    Crypto-asset transactions rely on blockchain networks operated by third parties. Network congestion, software bugs, hard forks, validator failures and similar events may delay, prevent or alter transactions.

  • Cybersecurity risk

    Crypto-assets and the means of access to them (private keys, credentials) are a target for cyber attacks. Even with strong controls, residual risk exists.

  • Liquidity risk

    The depth of the market for a given crypto-asset may not be sufficient to execute a transaction at the expected price, particularly in stress conditions.

  • Legal and regulatory risk

    The regulatory framework applicable to crypto-assets is evolving. Changes in law or regulation may affect the availability, cost or treatment of crypto-asset services.

  • Counterparty risk

    Where Hillgate acts as principal counterparty, clients are exposed to Hillgate's ability to perform its obligations. Hillgate maintains prudential safeguards in line with MiCAR.

  • Final and irreversible transactions

    On-chain transfers of crypto-assets are generally irreversible once confirmed. Errors in addresses, networks or amounts cannot easily be undone.

What this means for you

Crypto-asset services are not suitable for all clients. Hillgate provides services only to professional and business clients with the experience and capacity to assess the risks involved. Each client is responsible for its own assessment, controls and decisions.
