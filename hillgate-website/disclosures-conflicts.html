Conflicts of interest

Disclosure under Article 72(2) of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 (MiCAR).

Purpose of this disclosure

Hillgate is required to identify, prevent, manage and, where necessary, disclose conflicts of interest that may arise in the provision of its services. This page sets out the general nature and sources of those conflicts and the steps Hillgate takes to mitigate them. The disclosure is available at all times, on any device, in English.

Sources of conflicts of interest at Hillgate

  • Principal trading

    Hillgate acts as principal counterparty in its exchange service. The price Hillgate quotes includes a tiered markup over a published reference mid price. Hillgate's interest is in the markup; the client's interest is in execution.

  • Inventory management

    Hillgate may hold proprietary EMT inventory to enable principal trading. Movements in EMT inventory may interact with client flow.

  • Multiple client relationships

    Hillgate serves multiple institutional clients including market makers and exchanges. Client trades may, at times, be in opposing directions.

  • Affiliations

    Hillgate's minority shareholder Forte Securities Limited is itself a regulated brokerage. Forte Securities is not authorised to sell, market or promote Hillgate services and does not have access to Hillgate's client information.

  • Personnel ancillary roles

    Members of Hillgate's compliance and management functions may hold roles outside Hillgate. All such roles are pre-cleared and recorded in Hillgate's conflicts register.

Mitigation steps

  • Published reference price and tier markup, verifiable by any client without registration.
  • Three lines of defence governance with an independent Chief Compliance Officer reporting to the Supervisory Board.
  • Pre-clearance of ancillary positions and personal account dealing controls for all employees.
  • Internal conflicts register reviewed at least annually by the Chief Compliance Officer.
  • Personal account dealing prohibition for employees on the Hillgate platform.
  • Information barriers between client-facing functions and risk-bearing functions.
  • Annual training on conflicts of interest for all employees.

Reporting concerns

Clients and prospective clients who believe a conflict has not been adequately managed can raise this through our complaints procedure or, where appropriate, our whistleblowing channel.
