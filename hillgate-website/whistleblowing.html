Whistleblowing

Hillgate maintains an internal channel for raising concerns about potential breaches of law, regulation or internal policy. The channel is available to employees, contractors, suppliers, clients and other parties with a legitimate interest.

How to raise a concern

  • Internal channel: whistleblowing@hillgate.com
  • External channel: the Dutch Whistleblowers Authority (Huis voor Klokkenluiders) at huisvoorklokkenluiders.nl

Protection

Hillgate will not retaliate against any person who raises a concern in good faith. Confidentiality of the reporting party is protected, subject to applicable legal requirements.

What happens next

Reports are received by the Chief Compliance Officer (or, where the report concerns the CCO, by the Chair of the Supervisory Board). Reports are investigated and the reporting party is updated on the status of the investigation.
