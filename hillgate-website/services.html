How the two work together

The same client can hold fiat balances under the EMI and EMT balances under MiCAR, settle between them through the exchange service, and use the FX engine to move between fiat currencies.

Fiat balances held with Hillgate are e-money issued under our EMI licence. They are not bank deposits and are not covered by a deposit guarantee scheme. Funds are safeguarded in accordance with applicable rules. EMT balances held with Hillgate are crypto-assets held in custody under MiCAR through the segregated Stichting Custody Hillgate Finance, separate from Hillgate's own assets.
