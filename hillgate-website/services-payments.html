Payments and foreign exchange

Hillgate provides multi-currency named accounts, third-party fiat payments, foreign exchange and instant internal settlement to institutional and professional clients in the digital asset sector.

Provided by Hillgate Finance B.V. under its Electronic Money Institution licence. Authorised by De Nederlandsche Bank, register entry WFTEG R200389.
Hillgate provides services to professional and business clients only.

Named multi-currency accounts

Hillgate clients receive named virtual accounts in 14 currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, MXN, CHF, TRY, SGD, HKD, JPY, AED and others). Accounts are connected to relevant payment rails, including SEPA and SEPA Instant for EUR, FPS and CHAPS for GBP, FedWire and ACH for USD, and SWIFT where applicable.

Third-party payments

Clients can make and receive payments to and from third parties using their Hillgate accounts. Payments are subject to Hillgate's compliance and screening framework. Flat-fee pricing applies.

Foreign exchange

Clients can convert between supported fiat currencies. Quotes are firm at the moment of acceptance. Settlement is generally available within 15 minutes for liquid pairs.

Instant settlement network

Transfers between Hillgate clients settle instantly within Hillgate's ledger, twenty-four hours a day, every day of the year, including weekends and public holidays.

Important information about your funds with Hillgate

Funds held in your Hillgate account are e-money issued by Hillgate Finance B.V. under its Electronic Money Institution licence. They are not bank deposits and they are not protected by any deposit guarantee scheme. They are protected by Hillgate's safeguarding arrangements in accordance with the Wft (Dutch Financial Supervision Act) and the e-money safeguarding rules applied by De Nederlandsche Bank.
