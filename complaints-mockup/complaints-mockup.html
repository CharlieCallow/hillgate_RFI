Complaints

Submit a complaint.

If something hasn't gone the way it should, we want to hear from you. Hillgate Finance investigates every complaint we receive and aims to put things right quickly.

How to submit a complaint

You can reach our complaints team in three ways:

  • Using the secure web form below
  • By email to complaints@hillgate.finance
  • By post to Hillgate Finance B.V., Amsterdam, the Netherlands

Free of charge

Filing a complaint with Hillgate Finance is always free. We will never charge a fee for raising, investigating, or escalating a concern.

Languages

Complaints can be submitted and will be handled in English or Dutch. Replies will be issued in the language in which the complaint was filed.
